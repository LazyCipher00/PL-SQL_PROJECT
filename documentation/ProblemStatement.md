 # PROBLEM STATEMENT

Project Title: Crop Disease Alert Database

## PROBLEM:
Crop diseases are often misidentified or detected late, causing yield loss and reduced farmer income.

## CONTEXT:
Used by local agricultural extension officers and smallholder farmers to log symptoms and get instant disease suggestions at the district/community level.

## TARGET USERS:

Smallholder farmers (report symptoms)

Extension officers / Agronomists (review alerts & recommend treatment)

Agricultural program managers (monitor trends)

## PROJECT GOALS:

Automate matching of reported symptoms to known crop diseases using PL/SQL.

Generate alerts for matched and high-risk diseases and store audit logs.

Produce BI-ready data for trends, hotspots, and resource prioritization.

##  BI POTENTIAL:

KPIs: disease incidence by crop, top diseases, hotspot mapping by location, trend over time.

Dashboards to prioritize extension visits and allocate resources for high-risk outbreaks.
